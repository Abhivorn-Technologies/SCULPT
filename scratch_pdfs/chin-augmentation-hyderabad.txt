Balance your profile with improved chin projection
Chin augmentation improves the size, shape, or projection of the chin to create better
balance between the chin, nose, and rest of the face.

Understanding Chin Augmentation
Chin augmentation adds definition and projection to a weak or recessed chin, either through
a surgical implant or injectable filler, chosen based on the degree of change desired and
patient preference.
Many people considering Chin Augmentation have questions or feel a little unsure about
where to start — that's completely normal. Our team takes time during your first visit to
explain things in plain language, so you can make a confident, informed decision at your
own pace.
Signs You May Be a Good Candidate
You might be a good fit for this treatment if any of the following sound familiar:
Individuals with a naturally recessed or underprojected chin

Patients seeking better facial balance alongside a nose or jawline procedure
Those wanting either a temporary (filler) or permanent (implant) solution
The best way to know for sure is a one-on-one consultation, where your surgeon reviews
your health history and goals in detail.
How Chin Augmentation Works: Step by Step
Step 1: Consultation & Evaluation
You'll meet with your surgeon to discuss your goals, medical history, and concerns. This is
the time to ask questions and understand whether Chin Augmentation is right for you.
Step 2: Personalized Planning
Your surgeon examines your body structure and designs a plan for Chin Augmentation that
fits your anatomy, so results look natural and balanced.
Step 3: The Procedure
Chin Augmentation is performed in a modern, sterile operation theatre under appropriate
anesthesia, following strict safety protocols throughout.
Step 4: Recovery & Follow-Up
You're monitored closely after the procedure, with clear aftercare instructions and
scheduled follow-up visits to track healing and results.
Our Approach
After assessing facial proportions, your surgeon recommends either a chin implant (placed
through a small incision under the chin or inside the mouth for a permanent change) or
dermal filler (injected for a temporary, non-surgical enhancement), tailored to your goals.
Benefits

Recovery Timeline

Recovery looks a little different for everyone, but here's a general idea of what to
expect:
Filler-based augmentation has virtually no downtime; implant-based augmentation typically
involves 1-2 weeks of swelling before the final result becomes visible.
Your surgeon will give you a personalized recovery plan, along with clear guidance on
activity levels, medication, and follow-up visits, so healing stays on track.

Frequently Asked Questions
Is chin augmentation often done with rhinoplasty?
Yes, combining chin augmentation with rhinoplasty is common, since both work together to
balance overall facial profile.
How long do chin filler results last?
Filler-based chin augmentation typically lasts 12-18 months, after which a touch-up can
maintain the look.
Is the procedure painful?
Most patients feel minimal discomfort during Chin Augmentation itself, since it is performed

under anesthesia. Some soreness in the days after is normal and manageable with
prescribed medication.
How long does the surgery take?
This depends on the extent of the procedure and will be explained clearly during your
consultation, once your specific plan is finalized.
When will I see the final results?
You'll notice initial changes as swelling reduces over the first few weeks, with final results
becoming clearer over the following months.
Can a chin implant be removed later if I change my mind?
Yes, chin implants can be removed or replaced if desired, offering some flexibility over time.
