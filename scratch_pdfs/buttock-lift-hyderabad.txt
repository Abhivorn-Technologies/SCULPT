Firmer contour after weight loss or aging
A buttock lift removes excess, sagging skin from the buttock area — often after major weight
loss or with natural aging — to restore a firmer, more lifted contour.
At Sculpt Aesthetics in Madhapur, Hyderabad, Buttock Lift is performed by experienced
plastic surgeons Dr. Jagadish Kiran and Dr. Suma Sandhyala, using modern techniques
and a patient-first approach — so you always know what to expect, step by step.
Understanding Buttock Lift
Unlike a Brazilian Butt Lift (which adds volume through fat transfer), a buttock lift focuses on
removing excess loose skin and lifting the remaining tissue, making it especially suited to
patients whose main concern is sagging rather than lack of volume.
Many people considering Buttock Lift have questions or feel a little unsure about where to
start — that's completely normal. Our team takes time during your first visit to explain things
in plain language, so you can make a confident, informed decision at your own pace.
Signs You May Be a Good Candidate
You might be a good fit for this treatment if any of the following sound familiar:
Individuals with significant loose, sagging skin after major weight loss
Patients whose buttocks have lost firmness with age

Those whose main concern is excess skin rather than lack of volume

Our Approach
Excess skin is removed through a carefully planned incision, often along the upper buttock
crease, and the remaining tissue is lifted and secured for a firmer, smoother contour. This
can be combined with fat transfer if added volume is also desired.

Frequently Asked Questions
How is this different from a Brazilian Butt Lift?
A Brazilian Butt Lift (BBL) adds volume using fat transfer, while a buttock lift removes excess
skin to address sagging — some patients benefit from combining both.
Is this often done after major weight loss surgery?
Yes, it's commonly performed as part of a body-contouring plan following significant weight
loss.
Is the procedure painful?
Most patients feel minimal discomfort during Buttock Lift itself, since it is performed under

anesthesia. Some soreness in the days after is normal and manageable with prescribed
medication.
How long does the surgery take?
This depends on the extent of the procedure and will be explained clearly during your
consultation, once your specific plan is finalized.
When will I see the final results?
You'll notice initial changes as swelling reduces over the first few weeks, with final results
becoming clearer over the following months.
Can this be combined with a tummy tuck or thigh lift?
Yes, many patients combine it with other body-contouring procedures as part of a broader
post-weight-loss body lift plan.
