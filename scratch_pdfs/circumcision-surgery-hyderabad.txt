Circumcision involves surgical removal of the foreskin covering the tip of the penis,

Frequently Asked Questions
Will I need general anesthesia?
Most minor procedures like this are done comfortably under local anesthesia, so you stay
awake but feel no pain in the area.
Will there be a visible scar?
Incisions are small and carefully placed to minimize visible scarring, and they typically fade
well over time.
Is circumcision performed under general or local anesthesia?
This is usually performed under local anesthesia with sedation, or general anesthesia if
preferred, depending on patient comfort and surgeon recommendation.
How soon can I resume sexual activity after circumcision?
Most surgeons recommend waiting around 4-6 weeks for full healing before resuming sexual
activity; your surgeon will confirm based on your recovery.
