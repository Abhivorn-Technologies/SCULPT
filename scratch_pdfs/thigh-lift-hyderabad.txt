Firmer, smoother thighs after weight loss or aging
A thigh lift removes excess skin and tightens tissue in the thighs, addressing sagging that often develops
after significant weight loss or with age. At Sculpt Aesthetics in Madhapur, Hyderabad, Thigh Lift is
performed by experienced plastic surgeons Dr. Jagadish Kiran and Dr. Suma Sandhyala, using
modern techniques and a patient-first approach — so you always know what to expect, step by step.
Understanding Thigh Lift
Thigh lift surgery (thighplasty) tightens loose skin on the inner and/or outer thighs, often combined with
liposuction, to create a smoother, more proportionate leg contour.
Many people considering Thigh Lift have questions or feel a little unsure about where to start — that's
completely normal. Our team takes time during your first visit to explain things in plain language, so you
can make a confident, informed decision at your own pace.
Signs You May Be a Good Candidate
You might be a good fit for this treatment if any of the following sound familiar:
Individuals with loose, sagging inner or outer thigh skin
Patients following major weight loss with residual skin laxity
Those with good overall health and realistic expectations
The best way to know for sure is a one-on-one consultation, where your surgeon reviews your health
history and goals in detail.

How Thigh Lift Works: Step by Step
Step 1: Consultation & Evaluation
You'll meet with your surgeon to discuss your goals, medical history, and concerns. This is the time to ask
questions and understand whether Thigh Lift is right for you.
Step 2: Personalized Planning
Your surgeon examines your body structure and designs a plan for Thigh Lift that fits your anatomy, so
results look natural and balanced.
Step 3: The Procedure
Thigh Lift is performed in a modern, sterile operation theatre under appropriate anesthesia, following
strict safety protocols throughout.
Step 4: Recovery & Follow-Up
You're monitored closely after the procedure, with clear aftercare instructions and scheduled follow-up
visits to track healing and results.
Our Approach
Depending on where laxity is greatest, your surgeon may perform an inner thigh lift, an outer/lateral
thigh lift, or both, removing excess skin and often combining the procedure with liposuction for optimal
contouring.

Recovery Timeline
Recovery looks a little different for everyone, but here's a general idea of what to expect:
Most patients need 2-4 weeks before resuming normal activity, with compression garments worn to

Your surgeon will give you a personalized recovery plan, along with clear guidance on activity levels,

Scars & Marks
Scars are placed along the groin crease for inner thigh lifts, or along the outer thigh/hip for outer thigh
lifts, positioned to be as hidden as possible within natural body contours.
Safety & Things to Consider
Like any surgical procedure, this treatment carries general surgical risks, which your surgeon will explain
in detail during consultation. At Sculpt Aesthetics, we follow international safety standards, strict
infection-control protocols, and modern operation theatre facilities to keep every procedure as safe as
possible. A thorough medical evaluation before surgery helps make sure the procedure is right for you.
Frequently Asked Questions
Will there be a scar? Yes, scar length and placement depend on how much skin is removed; your
surgeon will discuss expected scarring and placement during consultation.
Can thigh lift be combined with liposuction? Yes, combining liposuction with a thigh lift is
common to further refine contour.
Is the procedure painful? Most patients feel minimal discomfort during Thigh Lift itself, since it is
performed under anesthesia. Some soreness in the days after is normal and manageable with prescribed
medication.
How long does the surgery take? This depends on the extent of the procedure and will be explained
clearly during your consultation, once your specific plan is finalized.
When will I see the final results? You'll notice initial changes as swelling reduces over the first few
weeks, with final results becoming clearer over the following months.
Is this often part of a full lower body lift? Yes, many patients combine a thigh lift with a tummy
tuck and/or buttock lift as part of a comprehensive post-weight-loss body contouring plan.
