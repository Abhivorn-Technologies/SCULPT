Understanding Blepharoplasty (Eyelid

Surgery)
Blepharoplasty is a surgical procedure that removes or repositions
excess skin, muscle, and fat from the upper and/or lower eyelids,
addressing droopiness, puffiness, and a tired appearance.
Many people considering Blepharoplasty (Eyelid Surgery) have
questions or feel a little unsure about where to start — that's completely
normal. Our team takes time during your first visit to explain things in
plain language, so you can make a confident, informed decision at your
own pace.
Signs You May Be a Good Candidate
You might be a good fit for this treatment if any of the following sound
familiar:
•  Individuals with sagging or droopy upper eyelids
•  Patients with under-eye bags or puffiness
•  Those whose vision is affected by excess upper eyelid skin
The best way to know for sure is a one-on-one consultation, where your
surgeon reviews your health history and goals in detail.
How Blepharoplasty (Eyelid Surgery) Works:
Step by Step
Step 1: Consultation & Evaluation
You'll meet with your surgeon to discuss your goals, medical history, and
concerns. This is the time to ask questions and understand whether
Blepharoplasty (Eyelid Surgery) is right for you.

Step 2: Personalized Planning
Your surgeon examines your body structure and designs a plan for
Blepharoplasty (Eyelid Surgery) that fits your anatomy, so results look
natural and balanced.
Step 3: The Procedure
Blepharoplasty (Eyelid Surgery) is performed in a modern, sterile
operation theatre under appropriate anesthesia, following strict safety
protocols throughout.
Step 4: Recovery & Follow-Up
You're monitored closely after the procedure, with clear aftercare
instructions and scheduled follow-up visits to track healing and results.
Our Approach
Depending on whether the upper eyelids, lower eyelids, or both are
treated, excess skin and fat are carefully removed or repositioned
through incisions placed along natural eyelid creases to minimize visible
scarring.
Benefits

✓ Improved upper peripheral vision in select cases

Recovery Timeline

Recovery looks a little different for everyone, but here's a general
idea of what to expect:
Swelling and bruising generally resolve within 1–2 weeks, with most

Your surgeon will give you a personalized recovery plan, along with

healing stays on track.

Scars & Marks
Incisions are placed along the natural creases of the upper eyelid or just
below the lash line on the lower eyelid, so scars are extremely well
concealed and fade further over time.
Safety & Things to Consider
Like any surgical procedure, this treatment carries general surgical risks,
which your surgeon will explain in detail during consultation. At Sculpt
Aesthetics, we follow international safety standards, strict infection-
control protocols, and modern operation theatre facilities to keep every
procedure as safe as possible. A thorough medical evaluation before
surgery helps make sure the procedure is right for you.

Frequently Asked Questions
Is the procedure painful? Most patients feel minimal discomfort during
Blepharoplasty (Eyelid Surgery) itself, since it is performed under
anesthesia. Some soreness in the days after is normal and manageable
with prescribed medication.
How long does the surgery take? This depends on the extent of the
procedure and will be explained clearly during your consultation, once
your specific plan is finalized.
When will I see the final results? You'll notice initial changes as
swelling reduces over the first few weeks, with final results becoming
clearer over the following months.
Can upper and lower eyelid surgery be done together? Yes, many
patients choose to treat both areas in a single session for a more
complete refresh, as advised by your surgeon.
Will eyelid surgery change how my eyes look permanently? Results
are long-lasting, though natural aging will continue; most patients enjoy
a refreshed appearance for many years.
