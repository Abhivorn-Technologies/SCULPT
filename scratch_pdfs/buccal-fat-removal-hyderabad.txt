Understanding Buccal Fat Removal
Buccal fat removal is a procedure that removes the buccal fat pads located in the lower
cheek area, reducing fullness and creating more defined cheekbones and a slimmer facial
appearance.
Many people considering Buccal Fat Removal have questions or feel a little unsure about
where to start — that's completely normal. Our team takes time during your first visit to
explain things in plain language, so you can make a confident, informed decision at your
own pace.

How Buccal Fat Removal Works: Step by Step
Step 1: Consultation & Evaluation

Our Approach
Performed through a small incision inside the mouth, the buccal fat pad is carefully removed
to achieve the desired degree of cheek slimming, tailored to complement each patient's
facial structure.

Additional Details You Should Know
How Much Fat Is Removed
Your surgeon removes a carefully judged amount of buccal fat — not too much — to preserve
natural facial harmony and avoid an overly hollowed look, especially important as faces
naturally lose volume with age.

Frequently Asked Questions
Who is suitable for buccal fat removal?
Patients with fuller cheeks seeking facial definition may benefit after evaluation.

Is the procedure painful?
Most patients feel minimal discomfort during Buccal Fat Removal itself, since it is performed
under anesthesia. Some soreness in the days after is normal and manageable with
prescribed medication.
How long does the surgery take?
This depends on the extent of the procedure and will be explained clearly during your
consultation, once your specific plan is finalized.
When will I see the final results?
You'll notice initial changes as swelling reduces over the first few weeks, with final results
becoming clearer over the following months.
Is buccal fat removal reversible?
No — buccal fat pads do not grow back once removed, so the results are considered
permanent, and this should be carefully considered before proceeding.
Will my face look hollow when I'm older?
This is why your surgeon is conservative with how much fat is removed — the goal is a
slimmer, more defined look that still ages gracefully.
