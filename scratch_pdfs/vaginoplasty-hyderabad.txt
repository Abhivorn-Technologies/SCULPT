Understanding Vaginoplasty
Vaginoplasty is a surgical procedure that tightens the vaginal canal and surrounding musculature,
addressing laxity that may develop after vaginal childbirth or as a natural part of aging.
Many people considering Vaginoplasty have questions or feel a little unsure about where to start — that's
completely normal. Our team takes time during your first visit to explain things in plain language, so you
can make a confident, informed decision at your own pace.

Frequently Asked Questions
Is the procedure painful? Most patients feel minimal discomfort during Vaginoplasty itself, since it is
performed under anesthesia. Some soreness in the days after is normal and manageable with prescribed
medication.
How long does the surgery take? This depends on the extent of the procedure and will be explained
clearly during your consultation, once your specific plan is finalized.
When will I see the final results? You'll notice initial changes as swelling reduces over the first few
weeks, with final results becoming clearer over the following months.
How long is the recovery period? Most patients need a few weeks of activity restriction, with a
longer period recommended before resuming intercourse, as advised by your surgeon.

Will this affect childbirth in the future? This is an important consideration your surgeon will
discuss with you, especially if you are planning future pregnancies.
