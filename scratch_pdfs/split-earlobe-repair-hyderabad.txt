Understanding Split Earlobe Repair
This procedure repairs a torn, split, or stretched earlobe by re-shaping and closing the affected tissue,
restoring a natural earlobe contour that can, if desired, be re-pierced later.
Many people considering Split Earlobe Repair have questions or feel a little unsure about where to start
— that's completely normal. Our team takes time during your first visit to explain things in plain
language, so you can make a confident, informed decision at your own pace.
Signs You May Be a Good Candidate
You might be a good fit for this treatment if any of the following sound familiar:
Individuals with a torn or split earlobe from injury or heavy earrings
Those with stretched piercings (gauges) they wish to restore
The best way to know for sure is a one-on-one consultation, where your surgeon reviews your health
history and goals in detail.

How Split Earlobe Repair Works: Step by Step
Step 1: Consultation & Diagnosis
Your surgeon examines the area and confirms whether Split Earlobe Repair is the right treatment for

The repair is designed to blend with the natural earlobe contour, and any resulting scar is typically very

Safety & Things to Consider
This is a minor procedure with a low overall risk profile when performed by an experienced surgeon
under sterile conditions. Your surgeon will still walk you through the small risks involved, such as

Frequently Asked Questions
Will I need general anesthesia? Most minor procedures like this are done comfortably under local
anesthesia, so you stay awake but feel no pain in the area.
Will there be a visible scar? Incisions are small and carefully placed to minimize visible scarring, and
they typically fade well over time.
How soon can I get my ears re-pierced after repair? Most surgeons recommend waiting about 6-
8 weeks for full healing before considering re-piercing.
Can both earlobes be repaired in one visit? Yes, both ears can usually be treated in the same short
procedure.
