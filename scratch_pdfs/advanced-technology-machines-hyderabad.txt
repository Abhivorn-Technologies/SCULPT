Our Approach
Lipo Vase (Ultrasound-Assisted Liposuction): Uses focused
ultrasound energy to gently liquefy fat before removal, allowing smoother
extraction, especially in fibrous areas like the male chest or back.
Vibra Sculpt (Power-Assisted Liposuction): Uses rapid, gentle
mechanical vibration on the liposuction cannula to loosen fat more
efficiently, reducing physical strain on tissue and often speeding up
recovery.
EMS Sculpt: A non-invasive electromagnetic muscle stimulation
technology used to tone and strengthen target muscle areas without
surgery — see our dedicated EMS Sculpt page for details.
InMode RF & Embrace RF: Radiofrequency-based technologies used
for non-surgical skin tightening and body contouring, stimulating
collagen production to improve skin laxity and texture.
Kegel Chair: A non-invasive, electromagnetic pelvic floor stimulation
device used to support pelvic floor strengthening — often relevant

alongside intimate aesthetic procedures or postpartum recovery.
