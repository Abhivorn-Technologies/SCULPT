Understanding Lip Augmentation / Reduction
Lip augmentation increases lip fullness and volume, while lip reduction decreases excess lip
volume — both aimed at achieving lips that are proportionate to the rest of the face.
Many people considering Lip Augmentation / Reduction have questions or feel a little unsure
about where to start — that's completely normal. Our team takes time during your first visit
to explain things in plain language, so you can make a confident, informed decision at your
own pace.

Frequently Asked Questions
Is the procedure painful?
Most patients feel minimal discomfort during Lip Augmentation / Reduction itself, since it is
performed under anesthesia. Some soreness in the days after is normal and manageable
with prescribed medication.
How long does the surgery take?
This depends on the extent of the procedure and will be explained clearly during your
consultation, once your specific plan is finalized.
When will I see the final results?
You'll notice initial changes as swelling reduces over the first few weeks, with final results
becoming clearer over the following months.
How long does lip filler last?
Lip fillers typically last 6-12 months, after which touch-ups can maintain your desired look.
Is surgical lip reduction permanent?
Yes, surgical lip reduction provides a long-term change compared to temporary filler-based

augmentation.
