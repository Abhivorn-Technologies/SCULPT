Understanding Medical Weight Loss Programs
Medical weight loss programs combine clinical evaluation, structured planning, and ongoing
supervision to support sustainable, healthy weight management, distinct from unsupervised
or fad approaches.
Many people considering Medical Weight Loss Programs have questions or feel a little unsure

to explain things in plain language, so you can make a confident, informed decision at your

Additional Details You Should Know

Understanding BMI in Weight Management
Body Mass Index (BMI) is one of the key measures used to assess weight-related health risk
and guide which weight-management approach — lifestyle-based, injectable-assisted, or
surgical body contouring — may suit you best. Your program is designed around your
individual BMI, health history, and goals.
Injectable Options & EMS Sculpt
For eligible patients, medically supervised weight-loss injectables (such as GLP-1 based
options) may be included as part of a structured program under medical guidance. We also
offer EMS Sculpt, a non-invasive muscle stimulation technology that can complement
weight loss by toning and strengthening muscle in areas like the abdomen and glutes.

Safety & Things to Consider
This is a non-surgical treatment with minimal downtime and a good safety profile when
performed by trained professionals using approved products. As with any injectable or
energy-based treatment, mild, temporary side effects such as redness or swelling are
possible and will be discussed during your consultation.
