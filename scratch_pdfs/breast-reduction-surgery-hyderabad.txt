Understanding Breast Reduction Surgery
Breast reduction (reduction mammaplasty) is a surgical procedure that removes excess
breast tissue, fat, and skin to reduce breast size and reshape the breasts for better
proportion and comfort.
Many people considering Breast Reduction Surgery have questions or feel a little unsure
about where to start — that's completely normal. Our team takes time during your first visit
to explain things in plain language, so you can make a confident, informed decision at your
own pace.
Signs You May Be a Good Candidate
You might be a good fit for this treatment if any of the following sound familiar:

Women experiencing back, neck, or shoulder discomfort from breast weight
Those with skin irritation or posture issues linked to breast size
Women facing difficulty with physical activity or clothing fit
Patients seeking better body proportion
The best way to know for sure is a one-on-one consultation, where your surgeon reviews
your health history and goals in detail.

Additional Details You Should Know
Mastopexy (Breast Lift) & Sagging
Breast reduction often naturally lifts the breast as part of the procedure, but in some cases a
dedicated breast lift (mastopexy) technique is combined to further correct sagging and
reposition the nipple for a more youthful, uplifted shape.
Addressing Asymmetry
For patients with uneven breast size alongside excess volume, your surgeon can tailor the
amount of tissue removed on each side to improve both size and symmetry in the same
procedure.

Scars & Marks
Scar pattern depends on the amount of reduction needed — commonly an anchor- or
lollipop-shaped incision is used, which fades considerably over the first year while still
delivering meaningfully lighter, better-shaped breasts.
Safety & Things to Consider

Like any surgical procedure, this treatment carries general surgical risks, which your surgeon

Frequently Asked Questions
Who is a candidate for breast reduction?
Women experiencing discomfort, oversized breasts, or difficulty with daily activities may
benefit from evaluation.
Will breast reduction leave visible scars?
Some scarring is expected with any surgical incision; technique and placement are chosen to
minimize visibility.
Is the procedure painful?
Most patients feel minimal discomfort during Breast Reduction Surgery itself, since it is
performed under anesthesia. Some soreness in the days after is normal and manageable
with prescribed medication.
How long does the surgery take?
This depends on the extent of the procedure and will be explained clearly during your
consultation, once your specific plan is finalized.
When will I see the final results?
You'll notice initial changes as swelling reduces over the first few weeks, with final results
becoming clearer over the following months.
Will breast reduction affect nipple sensation?
Some temporary changes in sensation are common after surgery and typically improve as
healing progresses; permanent changes are uncommon but will be discussed as part of
informed consent.
Can breast reduction help with back pain from large breasts?
Yes — relieving the physical weight and strain of oversized breasts is one of the most
common and effective benefits of this surgery.
