Frequently Asked Questions
Is the procedure painful?
Most patients feel minimal discomfort during Jawline Enhancement itself, since it is
performed under anesthesia. Some soreness in the days after is normal and manageable
with prescribed medication.
How long does the surgery take?
This depends on the extent of the procedure and will be explained clearly during your
consultation, once your specific plan is finalized.
When will I see the final results?
You'll notice initial changes as swelling reduces over the first few weeks, with final results
becoming clearer over the following months.
How long do filler-based jawline results last?
Filler results typically last 9-18 months depending on the product used and individual
metabolism, while surgical contouring offers longer-lasting change.
Is jawline enhancement suitable for both men and women?
Yes, treatment goals are tailored differently — often more angular for men and softly defined
for women — based on individual preference.
