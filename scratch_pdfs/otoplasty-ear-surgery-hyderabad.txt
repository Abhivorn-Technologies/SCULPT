Understanding Otoplasty (Ear Surgery)
Otoplasty is a surgical procedure that adjusts the size, shape, or position of the ears — most
commonly to set prominent ears closer to the head or correct asymmetry between the two
ears.
Many people considering Otoplasty (Ear Surgery) have questions or feel a little unsure about
where to start — that's completely normal. Our team takes time during your first visit to
explain things in plain language, so you can make a confident, informed decision at your
own pace.
Signs You May Be a Good Candidate
You might be a good fit for this treatment if any of the following sound familiar:
Individuals with ears that protrude more than they'd like

Those with noticeable asymmetry between the two ears
Both children (once ear cartilage has matured) and adults seeking correction
The best way to know for sure is a one-on-one consultation, where your surgeon reviews
your health history and goals in detail.

Our Approach
Through a small incision behind the ear, your surgeon reshapes the underlying cartilage to
achieve better proportion and position, taking care to preserve a natural fold and shape
rather than an overly 'pinned back' look.

Frequently Asked Questions
At what age can otoplasty be performed?
It can often be performed once ear cartilage has matured, sometimes as early as age 5-6 for
children, or at any age for adults.
Is otoplasty painful?
Discomfort is generally mild and manageable with prescribed pain relief in the days
following surgery.
Is the procedure painful?
Most patients feel minimal discomfort during Otoplasty (Ear Surgery) itself, since it is

performed under anesthesia. Some soreness in the days after is normal and manageable
with prescribed medication.
How long does the surgery take?
This depends on the extent of the procedure and will be explained clearly during your
consultation, once your specific plan is finalized.
When will I see the final results?
You'll notice initial changes as swelling reduces over the first few weeks, with final results
becoming clearer over the following months.
Are the results permanent?
Yes, results are generally permanent, as the cartilage is reshaped and typically holds its new
position long-term.
