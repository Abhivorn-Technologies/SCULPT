Understanding Belt Lipectomy
Belt lipectomy (circumferential body lift) removes a continuous band of
excess skin and fat around the entire waist and torso, lifting and
tightening the abdomen, flanks, and lower back together in one
coordinated procedure.
Many people considering Belt Lipectomy have questions or feel a little
unsure about where to start — that's completely normal. Our team takes
time during your first visit to explain things in plain language, so you can
make a confident, informed decision at your own pace.
Signs You May Be a Good Candidate
You might be a good fit for this treatment if any of the following sound
familiar:
•  Patients with significant excess skin encircling the entire
midsection, often after major weight loss
•  Those for whom a tummy tuck alone would not fully address the
back and flank areas
•  Individuals in good general health, at a stable weight, seeking a
comprehensive contouring solution
The best way to know for sure is a one-on-one consultation, where your
surgeon reviews your health history and goals in detail.

Frequently Asked Questions
How is this different from a tummy tuck? A tummy tuck addresses
the front of the abdomen, while a belt lipectomy treats the entire
circumference of the torso, including the back and flanks.
Is this typically done after bariatric surgery? Yes, it's a common and
often significant step in body-contouring plans following major weight
loss from bariatric surgery or other methods.
Is the procedure painful? Most patients feel minimal discomfort during
Belt Lipectomy itself, since it is performed under anesthesia. Some
soreness in the days after is normal and manageable with prescribed
medication.
How long does the surgery take? This depends on the extent of the
procedure and will be explained clearly during your consultation, once
your specific plan is finalized.
When will I see the final results? You'll notice initial changes as
swelling reduces over the first few weeks, with final results becoming
clearer over the following months.
Can this be combined with a thigh lift or arm lift? Yes, many patients
undergoing major-weight-loss body contouring combine belt lipectomy
with a thigh lift, arm lift, or breast procedure over a staged treatment
plan.
