Understanding Butt Lift & Body Sculpting
A butt lift enhances the shape, projection, and contour of the buttocks, most commonly
using fat transferred from other areas of the body (such as the abdomen or flanks) to create
natural, proportionate curves.
Many people considering Butt Lift & Body Sculpting have questions or feel a little unsure
about where to start — that's completely normal. Our team takes time during your first visit
to explain things in plain language, so you can make a confident, informed decision at your
own pace.

Additional Details You Should Know

Brazilian Butt Lift (BBL) vs. Buttock Lift
This page covers fat-transfer-based buttock enhancement, often referred to as a Brazilian
Butt Lift (BBL) — ideal for adding shape and volume. If your main concern is sagging skin (for
example after major weight loss) rather than lack of volume, a Buttock Lift (skin excision)
may be more suitable — ask your surgeon which approach fits you best.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the procedure painful?
Most patients feel minimal discomfort during Butt Lift & Body Sculpting itself, since it is
performed under anesthesia. Some soreness in the days after is normal and manageable
with prescribed medication.
How long does the surgery take?
This depends on the extent of the procedure and will be explained clearly during your
consultation, once your specific plan is finalized.
When will I see the final results?
You'll notice initial changes as swelling reduces over the first few weeks, with final results
becoming clearer over the following months.
How much fat is needed for a noticeable result?
This depends on your goals and available donor fat; your surgeon will assess this during
consultation and let you know what's realistic for your body.
How long before I can sit normally again?
Most patients are advised to avoid direct pressure on the buttocks for a period after surgery
to protect the transferred fat; your surgeon will give you a specific timeline.
