Understanding Labiaplasty
Labiaplasty is a surgical procedure that reshapes and, where needed, reduces the size of the
labia minora or labia majora, addressing discomfort, irritation, or aesthetic concerns.
Many people considering Labiaplasty have questions or feel a little unsure about where to
start — that's completely normal. Our team takes time during your first visit to explain things
in plain language, so you can make a confident, informed decision at your own pace.

Additional Details You Should Know
Techniques Used

Depending on individual anatomy, your surgeon may use a trim technique (removing excess
edge tissue) or a wedge technique (preserving the natural edge while reducing central bulk)
— both aim for natural appearance and function.

Frequently Asked Questions
Is the procedure painful?
Most patients feel minimal discomfort during Labiaplasty itself, since it is performed under
anesthesia. Some soreness in the days after is normal and manageable with prescribed
medication.

How long does the surgery take?
This depends on the extent of the procedure and will be explained clearly during your
consultation, once your specific plan is finalized.
When will I see the final results?
You'll notice initial changes as swelling reduces over the first few weeks, with final results
becoming clearer over the following months.
Will labiaplasty affect sexual sensation?
Techniques are chosen specifically to preserve normal sensation and function; this is
discussed thoroughly during your confidential consultation.
How long is the recovery period?
Most patients need about 1-2 weeks before resuming regular activities, with a longer period
advised before strenuous exercise or intercourse.
