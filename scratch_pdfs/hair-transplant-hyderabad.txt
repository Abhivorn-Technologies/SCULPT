Understanding Hair Transplantation
Hair transplantation involves relocating healthy hair follicles from a donor area (typically the
back of the scalp) to areas of thinning or baldness, providing a natural, permanent
restoration of hair growth.
Many people considering Hair Transplantation have questions or feel a little unsure about
where to start — that's completely normal. Our team takes time during your first visit to
explain things in plain language, so you can make a confident, informed decision at your
own pace.
Signs You May Be a Good Candidate
You might be a good fit for this treatment if any of the following sound familiar:
Men with male pattern baldness

Individuals experiencing hair thinning or a receding hairline
Patients with sufficient healthy donor hair
The best way to know for sure is a one-on-one consultation, where your surgeon reviews
your health history and goals in detail.
How Hair Transplantation Works: Step by Step
Step 1: Consultation & Hair Assessment
Your surgeon examines your scalp, hair density, and donor area to understand the cause and
extent of hair loss.
Step 2: Customized Planning
A treatment plan is designed around your natural hairline and the number of grafts needed
for a full, natural look.
Step 3: The Procedure
Healthy hair follicles are carefully moved from the donor area to thinning or bald areas,
following your natural hair growth direction.
Step 4: Recovery & Regrowth
You'll be guided through scalp care in the days after the procedure, and shown what to
expect as new hair grows in over the following months.
Our Approach
Follicles are carefully harvested from the donor area and transplanted individually into
thinning or bald areas, following each patient's natural hairline and hair growth pattern for a
seamless, undetectable result.

Scars & Marks
Modern follicular extraction techniques leave tiny, dot-like marks in the donor area that are
easily hidden by surrounding hair, without a single long linear scar.
Safety & Things to Consider
Hair transplantation is a well-established, minimally invasive procedure with a strong safety
record when performed by trained specialists. Your surgeon will review your scalp condition
and medical history to confirm you're a suitable candidate before proceeding.
Frequently Asked Questions
How long until I see new hair growth?
Transplanted hair usually sheds first, then regrows gradually, with visible new growth
typically starting around 3-4 months and continuing to improve over a year.
Is hair transplant a permanent solution?
Transplanted follicles are generally resistant to further hair loss, offering long-term, natural
results.
How many grafts will I need?
This depends on the extent of your hair loss and desired density, and will be estimated
during your scalp assessment.

Can women get hair transplants too?
Yes, hair transplantation can be suitable for certain patterns of female hair thinning,
following an individual evaluation.
