Understanding Tummy Tuck
Abdominoplasty is a surgical procedure that removes excess skin and fat from the abdomen while
tightening underlying weakened or separated abdominal muscles for a smoother, firmer contour.
Many people considering Tummy Tuck have questions or feel a little unsure about where to start — that's
completely normal. Our team takes time during your first visit to explain things in plain language, so you
can make a confident, informed decision at your own pace.
Signs You May Be a Good Candidate
You might be a good fit for this treatment if any of the following sound familiar:
Individuals with loose abdominal skin after pregnancy
Patients following significant weight loss
Those with weakened abdominal muscles unresponsive to exercise
The best way to know for sure is a one-on-one consultation, where your surgeon reviews your health
history and goals in detail.

How Tummy Tuck Works: Step by Step
Step 1: Consultation & Evaluation
You'll meet with your surgeon to discuss your goals, medical history, and concerns. This is the time to ask
questions and understand whether Tummy Tuck is right for you.
Step 2: Personalized Planning
Your surgeon examines your body structure and designs a plan for Tummy Tuck that fits your anatomy,
so results look natural and balanced.
Step 3: The Procedure
Tummy Tuck is performed in a modern, sterile operation theatre under appropriate anesthesia, following
strict safety protocols throughout.
Step 4: Recovery & Follow-Up
You're monitored closely after the procedure, with clear aftercare instructions and scheduled follow-up
visits to track healing and results.

Frequently Asked Questions
Is tummy tuck a weight loss surgery? No. It is a body contouring procedure, not a method for losing
weight.
Is the procedure painful? Most patients feel minimal discomfort during Tummy Tuck itself, since it is
performed under anesthesia. Some soreness in the days after is normal and manageable with prescribed
medication.
How long does the surgery take? This depends on the extent of the procedure and will be explained
clearly during your consultation, once your specific plan is finalized.
When will I see the final results? You'll notice initial changes as swelling reduces over the first few
weeks, with final results becoming clearer over the following months.

Will a tummy tuck remove stretch marks? Stretch marks located on the skin that is removed will
be eliminated; those on remaining skin will remain, though the overall abdominal appearance is often
significantly improved.
Can I have more children after a tummy tuck? It's generally recommended to complete your
family before this surgery, as pregnancy afterward can affect the results and may require revision.
