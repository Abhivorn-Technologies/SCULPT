Understanding Double Chin Treatment
A double chin refers to excess fat and/or loose skin under the chin, which can occur due to
genetics, weight, or ageing. Several treatment options exist depending on the underlying
cause.
Many people considering Double Chin Treatment have questions or feel a little unsure about
where to start — that's completely normal. Our team takes time during your first visit to
explain things in plain language, so you can make a confident, informed decision at your
own pace.

Frequently Asked Questions
Is the procedure painful?
Most patients feel minimal discomfort during Double Chin Treatment itself, since it is
performed under anesthesia. Some soreness in the days after is normal and manageable
with prescribed medication.
How long does the surgery take?
This depends on the extent of the procedure and will be explained clearly during your
consultation, once your specific plan is finalized.
When will I see the final results?
You'll notice initial changes as swelling reduces over the first few weeks, with final results

becoming clearer over the following months.
Can double chin treatment be combined with a facelift or jawline procedure?
Yes, many patients combine chin liposuction or skin tightening with jawline contouring or a
facelift for a more complete lower-face result.
Are non-surgical options as effective as liposuction?
Non-surgical treatments work well for mild fullness, while liposuction typically gives a more
definitive result for more significant fat deposits — your surgeon will recommend based on
your case.
